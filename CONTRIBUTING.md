# Contributing to ColdSnap

Thank you for contributing to ColdSnap.

Before a contribution can be merged, its contributor must accept the
[ColdSnap Contributor License Agreement](CLA.md) through a contribution process
designated by the Project Owners. The CLA does not transfer ownership of a
contribution; it grants the rights described in the agreement.

When submitting a contribution:

- submit only work you authored or are authorized to contribute;
- identify third-party material and its source and license;
- preserve applicable copyright, SPDX, attribution, and license notices; and
- run `make check` and any relevant external qualification tests.

The repository does not yet designate a particular electronic CLA acceptance
service. Until one is documented, coordinate acceptance with the Project Owners.
